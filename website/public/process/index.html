Job site: piers to framed house

The Pier Beam drives the foundation while the trailer line starts building panels. The crane sets girders as soon as each pier row is done, then every panel as it comes off the line.

Choose the house size and pier spacing, then press Start.

Drag to orbit. Right-drag or two fingers to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom toward the pointer; double-click to center. Arrow keys pan, + / − zoom.
