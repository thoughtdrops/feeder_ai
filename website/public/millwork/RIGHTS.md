# Source and rights

All 100 profile thumbnails preserve original catalog scan pixels from The Rockwell Manufacturing Co., Millwork Catalogue (1904), digitized by North Carolina State University Libraries and hosted by Internet Archive. This United States publication predates 1930 and is in the US public domain.

Source item: https://archive.org/details/millworkcatalogu00rock
Source PDF: https://archive.org/download/millworkcatalogu00rock/millworkcatalogu00rock.pdf

Numbered cross-sections: 8000-8034 (printed 233-236), 8283-8332 (printed 255-261), and 8414-8428 (printed 274-275). These 100 numbered drawings comprise 24 crown, 6 sprung cove, 5 sprung bed, 5 drip cap, 45 casing, and 15 base sections (including base caps and shoes). The inventory records the matching printed and PDF page numbers.

The original page renders and original rectangular crops are included separately. Thumbnail masking only blanks neighboring drawing pixels outside a section perimeter; interior scan pixels are preserved. Sections use distinct source-vectorized outlines, with scan-line gaps repaired conservatively. Drafting zigzags in long base boards represent length breaks; their flat fields are reconstructed.

Coordinates are normalized outward projection and height above the installed bottom. Casing cuts are rotated upright; drip caps place the raised lip against the wall; sprung beds are reflected into their installed spring orientation. Catalog nominal width and thickness are read from the labeled drawings. Any uncertain fraction is explicitly marked dimensionsApproximate. Crown/cove/bed installation height and projection are approximate estimates from the illustrated spring geometry and nominal board width, marked installationDimensionsApproximate. These historical catalog sections are reference geometry, not fabrication drawings.
